Quick tips

  • Photos stay on your phone — Pix Mystery does not upload your library to its own servers.
  • Restore purchases — Settings → Restore purchase (same Apple ID used to subscribe).
  • Manage subscription — iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Ads on Free — Yandex interstitial ads may appear between sessions; Artist mode removes them.
  • Clear library — Settings → Clear library deletes local projects on this device (cannot be undone).
